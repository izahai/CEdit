# TGPRS style-subspace comparison: rank 30 vs. rank 100

This report compares the two style-suffixed TGPRS evaluations recorded in:

- [`../style_suffix/0.3_report_17179_style_suffix.md`](../style_suffix/0.3_report_17179_style_suffix.md), using 30 anchors and rank 30;
- [`../style_100r/0.3_report_18802_style_100r.md`](../style_100r/0.3_report_18802_style_100r.md), using 100 anchors and rank 100.

Both runs use original Stable Diffusion v1.4, target prompts suffixed by
`style`, threshold `0.3`, `retain_scale=1.0`, `aug_num=0`, anchor `art`, seed 0,
DPM-Solver, 20 denoising steps, and CFG 7.5. Each artist metric contains 30
images and each MS-COCO metric contains 100 images. The original and SPEED
results are identical between runs, so the comparison below isolates the TGPRS
subspace change.

Lower target CLIP score indicates stronger erasure. Lower non-target and
MS-COCO FID and higher MS-COCO CLIP score indicate better preservation. Bold
values are better between the two TGPRS configurations. Target FID is reported
without assigning a preferred direction.

## Rank-100 change relative to rank 30

Positive target-CS and FID deltas are increases; negative values are decreases.

| Task | Δ Target CS | Δ Target FID | Δ Non-target FID | Δ MS-COCO CS | Δ MS-COCO FID |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Van Gogh | +0.151 | +1.74 | +4.18 | -0.281 | +1.59 |
| Picasso | +0.381 | +8.10 | +3.54 | -0.053 | -1.46 |
| Monet | -0.301 | +3.68 | +4.92 | +0.243 | +0.08 |

Rank 100 improves erasure only for Monet, lowering its target CLIP score by
0.301. It weakens erasure for Van Gogh and Picasso by increasing their target
scores by 0.151 and 0.381. Non-target FID becomes worse for every task. The
MS-COCO effects are mixed: rank 100 improves Picasso's FID and Monet's CLIP
score, but worsens the other task-metric combinations.

## Conclusion

Increasing the anchor set and residual rank from 30 to 100 does not improve the
overall tradeoff in this evaluation. Rank 30 has a slightly lower average target
CLIP score, a 4.21-point lower mean non-target FID, a slightly higher MS-COCO
CLIP score, and a slightly lower MS-COCO FID. Rank 100's main benefit is the
0.301-point target-score improvement for Monet, but that gain is accompanied by
worse non-target FID for Monet and weaker aggregate preservation.

For these three artists and this sampling budget, rank 30 is the stronger default.
